About Categories

Categories help you organise your financial activities into meaningful groups. They make it easier to understand where your money comes from and where it goes.

Category Types

Income Categories

Organise sources of money you receive, such as salaries, investments and business income.

Salary Freelance Business

Expense Categories

Group the things you spend money on, such as food, transportation, rent and utilities.

Food Transport Rent

Why Categories Matter

  • Understand your spending habits.
  • Identify your major income sources.
  • Get more meaningful financial reports.
  • Organise transactions more effectively.

Helpful Tips

  • Keep category name short, specific and easy to recognise.
  • Use clear names that describe each financial activity.
  • Ensure category type matches its purpose.
  • Add more details to match category names.

About Budgets

Budgets help you set spending limits for specific categories over a defined period. They give you better control over your expenses and help you make more informed financial decisions.

How Budgets Work

  • 01
    Set a spending limit

    Decide how much you want to spend within a particular category.

  • 02
    Track your expenses

    Record transactions assigned to the budget's category.

  • 03
    Monitor your progress

    See how much you've spent and how much remains.

Important Information

Ensure your start and end dates match your selected budget period. This helps keep your budget tracking accurate.

Helpful Tips

  • Set realistic spending limits.
  • Review your budgets regularly.
  • Avoid setting budgets beyond your means.
  • Ensure budget end date is greater than its start date.
