About Transactions

Track the money coming in and going out of your finances. Transactions help you monitor your financial activity and keep your records up to date.

Income

Money you receive, such as salaries or business earnings.

Expenses

Money you spend on things like food, transportation or rent.

Required Information

  • Transaction Title – Give your transaction a recognisable name.

  • Transaction Type – Specify whether money was received or spent.

  • Amount – Enter the amount involved.

  • Category – Organise your transaction under a relevant category.

  • Transaction Date – Keep your financial records accurate.

Recurring Transactions

Ideal for regular financial activities that happen on a predictable schedule, such as monthly rent, subscriptions or salary payments.

Tip: Enabling recurring transactions requires you to specify both a Repeat Frequency and a Next Due Date.

Helpful Tips

  • Choose the right category.
  • Choose unique transaction title.
  • Be accurate with the amount.
  • Use recurring transactions for predictable payments.
